Why Constraints Make Better Pages

Limitations are design catalysts in disguise.

Apr 18, 2026Design Notes5 min read

A good constraint narrows the field until the important choices become visible. It makes the page less arbitrary and more memorable.

For a blog, constraints can live in the grid, palette, typography, asset set, or publishing rhythm.

Rules create recognizable rhythm

Repeated spacing, card proportions, and image treatments let visitors learn the system quickly.

Once the rhythm is established, a deliberate exception feels expressive rather than accidental.

Constraints are the quiet rails that make expression legible.

Limits protect the reader

Too many treatments make scanning expensive. A bounded system lets the content carry more of the attention.
